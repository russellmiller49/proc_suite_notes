THERAPEUTIC BRONCHOSCOPY
PRE: Mucus plugging with lobar atelectasis (J98.11)
POST: Same, improved
Procedure: Flexible bronchoscopy with therapeutic aspiration; bronchial toileting (codes sometimes: 31645)
Sedation: Moderate sedation with topical lidocaine
Complications: None

Findings/Technique:
Bronchoscopy via oral approach. Copious tenacious secretions obstructing right lower lobe basilar segments; suctioning and saline aliquots used to clear plugs. Airways re-expanded visually with improved ventilation to segmental level. No endobronchial lesion.

Specimens:

Bronchial washings from RLL for culture.

Plan:
Continue pulmonary hygiene; consider repeat imaging in 24–48 hours; follow cultures.
