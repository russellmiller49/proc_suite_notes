Pleuroscopy with biopsy ± talc pleurodesis (narrative)

Patient: [PATIENT], 72-year-old female with recurrent right pleural effusion and pleural nodularity on CT.

Procedure: Medical pleuroscopy with pleural biopsies and talc pleurodesis.
Anesthesia: Monitored anesthesia care.
Complications: None.

Technique/Findings:
After ultrasound localization, single port entry into right pleural space. Approximately 900 mL serosanguinous fluid aspirated. Pleuroscopic inspection revealed diffuse pleural studding over parietal pleura. Biopsies obtained from multiple sites. Talc poudrage performed evenly over pleural surfaces. A chest tube was placed and connected to suction.

Specimens:
“Right pleural biopsy” (multiple)

Plan:
Chest tube management per protocol; pain control; follow pathology and cytology.
