Therapeutic bronchoscopy — clot extraction

Patient: [PATIENT], 57-year-old female with recent hemoptysis and airway clot on CT.

Procedure: Bronchoscopy with clot extraction and airway clearance.
Anesthesia: General anesthesia.
Complications: None.

Findings:
Organized clot partially obstructing left lower lobe bronchus.

Intervention:
Clot removed using forceps and suction. Cold saline applied for minor oozing; no further bleeding. Airways patent to segmental level.

Specimens: None.
Plan: Hemoptysis precautions; follow imaging and underlying cause workup.
