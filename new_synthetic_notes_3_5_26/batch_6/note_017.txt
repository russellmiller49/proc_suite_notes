PRE-PROCEDURE DX: Tracheal stenosis after prolonged intubation
POST-PROCEDURE DX: Same

Procedure(s):
Rigid bronchoscopy, balloon dilation, cryotherapy to granulation tissue
CPT-ish: [31630]; [31641?]

Indication:
Progressive stridor and dyspnea.

Findings:
Subglottic/proximal tracheal stenosis with granulation tissue, residual lumen approximately 5 mm. Distal trachea normal.

Technique:
Under general anesthesia with jet ventilation, rigid bronchoscopy performed. Granulation tissue treated with cryotherapy. Balloon dilation carried out gradually to 12 mm with improved airway caliber.

Specimens:
None.

Complications:
Transient desaturation during dilation, resolved with ventilation pause and re-oxygenation.

Plan:
Observe overnight. Continue reflux precautions and follow-up airway evaluation.
