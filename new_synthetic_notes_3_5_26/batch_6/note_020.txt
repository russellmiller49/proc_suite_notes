Procedure:
Intrapleural fibrinolytic instillation via existing right pleural catheter.

Indication:
Loculated empyema with poor drainage.

Technique:
Existing 14 Fr catheter inspected and flushed. Alteplase 10 mg in 30 mL saline followed by DNase 5 mg in 30 cc saline instilled through the pleural tube; tube clamped for 60 mins. Dressing clean and intact.

Findings:
Catheter patent. Prior drainage thick yellow fluid. No resistance to instillation.

Specimens:
None newly obtained.

Complications:
Mild pleuritic discomfort during instillation.

Plan:
Unclamp after dwell time, return to suction, monitor output and pain.
