Procedure note:
Robotic bronchoscopy with PEF ablation.

Indication:
Biopsy-proven peripheral adenocarcinoma in the left lower lobe, treatment session.

Technique:
Robotic bronchoscopy performed to a left lower lobe lesion using ION platform. Cone-beam CT used for confirmation of catheter position within the target. Pulsed electric field ablation delivered per planned treatment cycle. Post-treatment CT spin showed expected peri-lesional change. Track treatment performed on withdrawal.

Findings:
No central endobronchial lesion. Stable target access achieved. No major bleeding.

Specimens:
None, diagnosis previously established.

Complications:
Small left pneumothorax was not seen immediately after treatment but a trace apical pneumothorax was noted on recovery film without symptoms.

Plan:
Observation with repeat chest radiograph; discharge if stable.
