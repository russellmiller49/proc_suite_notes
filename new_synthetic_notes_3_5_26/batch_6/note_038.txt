PRE-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS
Right middle lobe peripheral lesion, previously diagnosed, planned bronchoscopic cryoablation.

POST-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS
Same, status post cryoablation.

PROCEDURES
Navigational bronchoscopy with Galaxy-assisted guidance
Cone-beam CT confirmation
Cryoablation of peripheral lesion
Track ablation

FINDINGS
Guided access achieved to right middle lobe target. Imaging confirmed treatment catheter position within the lesion. Expected ice-ball related treatment effect visualized on intraprocedural imaging. No significant endobronchial bleeding.

DETAILS
Following guided bronchoscopy to the lesion, cryoablation treatment was delivered. Catheter position was confirmed before treatment and reassessed after ablation. Track ablation performed during withdrawal.

SPECIMENS
None.

COMPLICATIONS
None immediate.

PLAN
Post-procedure chest x-ray and short observation period.
