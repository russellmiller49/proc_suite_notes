PRE-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS
Need for evaluation of tracheostomy position after difficult exchange at bedside.

POST-PROCEDURE DIAGNOSIS
New tube in appropriate position.

PROCEDURE
Flexible bronchoscopy through tracheostomy.

FINDINGS
Cuffed tracheostomy tube centered within tracheal lumen, distal tip 2 cm above carina. Mild posterior wall irritation without laceration. Both main bronchi patent with scant secretions suctioned clear.

PROCEDURE IN DETAIL
Bronchoscope was inserted through the tracheostomy tube for confirmation of placement and inspection after recent exchange. No false passage seen.

SPECIMENS
None.

COMPLICATIONS
None immediate.

PLAN
Routine trach care and wean FiO2 as tolerated.
