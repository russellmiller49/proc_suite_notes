Admit to monitored bed, serial chest radiographs, and bronchodilator therapy.

RUL lesion with mediastinal adenopathy; diagnosis and staging completed.

Enlarging right upper lobe lesion with PET-avid 4R node.

FINDINGS
No central endobronchial tumor. Robotic scope articulation allowed access to the posterior segment target. Three fiducials were deployed after tissue acquisition. Linear EBUS identified enlarged 4R and 7 nodes. After airway inspection, robotic bronchoscopy was used to access the lesion. Samples obtained included TBNA x4, forceps biopsy x6, and cryobiopsy x2. Minor bleeding after cryobiopsy resolved with tamponade and suction. EBUS-TBNA then performed at 4R x5 and 7 x3. ROSE from peripheral and nodal samples suggested adequacy.
